Important Information About Prescription Medicines

At Marnie Aesthetics, your safety and wellbeing are our highest priorities. Some of the treatments we offer may involve the use of prescription medicines. New Zealand law regulates the promotion of these medicines.

Consultation Required
Prescription medicines are only available following a consultation with a qualified health practitioner.
Not all treatments are suitable for every individual.
Risks and Benefits
Like all medicines, prescription medicines may have benefits and risks.
Your practitioner will discuss these with you during your consultation before any treatment is provided.
Suitability
The decision to prescribe any medicine will be based on your individual medical history, concerns, treatment goals and clinical assessment.
More Information
Consumer Medicine Information (CMI) is available on request.
Please speak with your practitioner if you would like further information about any medicine used during your treatment.
Individual Results Vary
Results from cosmetic treatments vary between individuals.
No specific outcomes can be guaranteed.

Questions?

Please speak with your practitioner during your consultation if you have any questions regarding prescription medicines or treatment suitability.

Accordions & Expandable Details

FAQ Accordion (.faq-list details with + / – Toggle)

Do I need a consultation before my first treatment?
Yes. Thorough consultations allow us to review medical history, assess facial anatomy, discuss your aesthetic goals, and construct a tailored treatment plan.
Will my results look natural?
The Marnie Aesthetics ethos is built entirely upon subtle enhancements that refresh and elevate your natural beauty without drastic alterations.
What is the recommended aftercare and downtime?
Most injectable treatments have minimal downtime. We provide bespoke post-treatment guidelines and support for every client.

Prescription Medicine Disclosure Item (.pmi-item)

Consultation Required
Prescription medicines are only available following a consultation with a qualified health practitioner. Not all treatments are suitable for every individual.
